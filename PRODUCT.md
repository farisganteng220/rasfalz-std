## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** Creative clients, content creators, business owners, and commissioners evaluating Raihan Salman Alfarisy (Rasfalz Studio) for motion graphics, graphic design, 3D artworks, video editing, and visual branding projects. They explore the site to inspect portfolio pieces, assess aesthetic capabilities, check availability/rates, or initiate contact via WhatsApp/Telegram.

**Secondary:** Digital creators and video editors seeking editing presets (Alight Motion, CapCut, Canva, After Effects, Blender 3D), sound effects, and master project assets via Payhip, Shopee, and SociaBuzz.

**Tertiary:** Creative community members and supporters who participate in the WhatsApp/Telegram resource groups, access weekly free presets/tutorials, or send donations via Trakteer, Saweria, SociaBuzz, or QRIS.

## Product Purpose

A personal digital operating system and multi-channel creative hub. The product elevates Raihan's portfolio from a static showcase into a memorable, interactive OS experience (featuring simulated Desktop Chrome OS and Android Mobile environments). It bridges discovery, proof-of-craft, digital asset commerce, community channels, and direct inquiry pathways into a unified, high-performance interface. Success means visitors experience Raihan's motion and visual design mastery firsthand, discover relevant works effortlessly, and convert into clients, store customers, or community members.

## Positioning

An immersive, dual-metaphor digital operating system ("Rasfalz Studio OS") that functions both as a showcase of 10+ years of creative expertise and as an interactive demonstration of frontend craft. Unlike standard portfolio templates or generic link-in-bio pages, the OS interface actively showcases motion design rhythm, glassmorphism aesthetics, system widgets, and interactive simulations across desktop and mobile form factors.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive via social media profiles (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, GitHub), referral links, or community channels. Typical sessions range from 3 to 15 minutes across desktop and mobile devices. Typical user actions include:
- Exploring visual portfolios and design galleries (Graphic Design, 3D Renders, Motion Graphics, Master Project Files)
- Navigating external portfolio archives (Foriio portfolios, Google Drive cloud storage)
- Purchasing digital goods and editing assets (Payhip, Shopee, SociaBuzz)
- Joining creative channels and downloading free preset packs (WhatsApp Channel, Telegram Channel)
- Sending direct project inquiries or commission requests via WhatsApp / Telegram
- Streaming curated playlist tracks via the built-in floating vinyl music player and audio visualizer
- Playing interactive mini-games (Snake Game) or experimenting with the camera/photo booth app

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Capabilities:**
- Dual OS Simulation: Desktop workspace (shelf dock, system top bar, draggable window management, calendar/clock widgets, system control center) and Mobile Android workspace (status bar, Google search pill, app drawer, navigation dock)
- 15+ interactive app modules: Portfolio Hub, Visual Gallery, News & Updates, Services & Commissions, Shop / Marketplace, Donations & Support, Community Channels, Music Player with Audio Visualizer, Snake Game, File Explorer, Visual Identity & Brand Guide, Camera & Filters
- Dynamic dark & light theme modes with curated glassmorphism tokens (Dark `#191919` Matte Charcoal / Light `#F8F8FF` Ghost White)
- Bilingual copy foundations (Indonesian primary with English interface conventions)
- Seamless external integrations for instant chat (WhatsApp direct API, Telegram direct link), store gateways, and multi-channel tip jars

**Constraints:**
- Operating system design metaphor (Desktop Dock / Mobile Android) is a fundamental brand signature and must remain intact across all views
- Commission intake status is currently explicitly marked as "⛔️ Close for Commissions & Collaborations" in config, while showcasing active portfolio capabilities
- Desktop and mobile experiences must retain full functional and content parity

## Product Principles

1. **OS as the Ultimate Proof of Craft:** The interactive OS interface is not mere gimmick—it acts as living evidence of Raihan's attention to motion, layout rhythm, interactive responsiveness, and visual polish.
2. **Frictionless Multi-Channel Pathways:** Make every client journey effortless—whether discovering past motion work, purchasing editing presets, streaming music, or contacting via WhatsApp.
3. **True Dual-Form Parity:** Treat Desktop OS and Mobile Android simulations with equal visual rigor and polish, ensuring mobile users receive an authentic, tailored interface rather than a degraded responsive collapse.
4. **Balanced High-Energy Aesthetics:** Balance bold, energetic accents (orange & electric blue) with clean glass surfaces, strict typography hierarchy, 2px borders, and deliberate micro-interactions without sensory overload.
5. **Authentic Creator Identity:** Center real project milestones (1000+ projects, 10+ years, 100+ clients), real Indonesian creator workflows, and direct contact channels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Responsive touch targets (minimum 44px on mobile navigation dock and pill buttons)
- Bilingual navigation labels and intuitive iconography for effortless browsing
